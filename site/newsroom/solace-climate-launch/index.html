Case study

Solace had a platform that did something new and a name nobody had heard of. The work was to define the category first, then build the brand that could lead it.

Technical founders tend to describe what a product does. Buyers want to know what problem is now solved that was not solved before. Solace needed the second sentence.

Name the category, then own it

We wrote the category definition before touching the identity: what it replaces, who it is for and why the timing is right. That sentence became the spine of the pitch deck, the website and the launch film.

An identity built for daylight

Climate brands default to green and leaves. Solace needed to look like infrastructure people would trust with a budget. Warm architectural forms, translucent materials and landscape light gave it a world that reads as optimistic without the cliches.

Launch as a system

The launch toolkit covered a keynote, a product site, an investor narrative and a sales deck, all built from one set of components. Six months on, the team is still shipping from it.
