Privacy Policy

Last updated September 2026

01What we collect

We collect the details you choose to share through the project enquiry form and the newsletter sign-up: your name, work email, company and the message you write. We do not buy data about you from third parties.

02How we use it

We use your details to answer your enquiry, to arrange a first conversation and, if you signed up, to send studio notes. We do not sell or rent personal information.

03Who we share it with

Form submissions are processed by the provider configured for this site. Newsletter sign-ups are stored with the email service we use. Both act on our instructions and under their own privacy terms.

04How long we keep it

Enquiry details are kept for as long as the conversation is active and for a reasonable period afterwards. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

05Your rights

You can ask what information we hold about you, request a correction or ask for it to be deleted. Write to the studio email listed on the contact page and we will respond within a month.

06Cookies

This site does not set tracking cookies. If analytics are added later, this policy will be updated to describe them.
