Museum Product Discovery Program

Museum Product Discovery Program

Choose products in the real world — not from a PDF. Explore a working showroom, handle the samples and discover what your museum shop could become.

Explore first. Decide afterwards.

See what we cannot show online. Touch what cannot be understood from a catalogue. Build what does not exist yet. We review your shop and goals before the visit, then invite selected museum teams into a large factory and wholesale showroom to compare real products, materials, finishes and packaging. Afterwards, you receive a tailored catalogue of the items you chose, with photos, specifications, minimum order quantities, prices and custom design options. There is no obligation to purchase.

A sourcing visit shaped around your museum

[01]

Shop Review

Share your visitor profile, current assortment, space, price points and collection themes so we can prepare a relevant visit.

[02]

Physical Discovery

Walk through extensive product ranges and compare real objects side by side rather than choosing from catalogue images.

[03]

Materials & Methods

Handle samples and examine production techniques, finishes, packaging formats and display possibilities.

[04]

Custom Development

Explore how selected formats could be adapted to your collection, visual identity or exhibition programme.

[05]

Personalised Catalogue

Receive your selected items with photographs, specifications, MOQ, indicative prices and customisation options for review at home.

[06]

No Purchase Obligation

Participation does not require an order. For selected institutions, reasonable travel costs may be covered when approved in advance and permitted by their internal rules.
